## 🧭 Who I Am

I'm a **Fractional CTO** with deep roots in **Kubernetes, MLOps, and Cybersecurity** — helping AI startups and SaaS companies scale securely across **Azure · AWS · OCI**.

But I'm not just an architect. I'm a builder, trainer, and community catalyst who believes that technology's greatest value lies in the hands of people who truly understand it.

Before going deep into cloud infrastructure, I organized treks to remote Himalayan villages, co-founded an AgriTech startup, and collaborated with NGOs across India. That journey gave me something no certification ever could — **the ability to stay grounded and creative when complexity peaks.**

I'm self-taught, powered by open source, Harvard OpenCourseWare, MIT, Stanford, Coursera, and the YouTube rabbit holes that most engineers know all too well.
